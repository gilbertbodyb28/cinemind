# Code of Conduct

CineMind should be welcoming, factual and technically constructive. Treat people with respect regardless of background, identity, experience or opinion.

Expected behavior: be respectful and specific, welcome beginner questions, protect private data, give accurate credit and distinguish facts from proposals.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, publishing private information, spam, fake stars, fake contributors, fabricated testimonials, manipulated statistics and misrepresenting maintainer or core-team status.

Report serious or private conduct concerns through the private channel described in SECURITY.md. Maintainers may remove content or reject contributions that violate this code.
