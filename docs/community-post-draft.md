# Community post draft — do not publish without approval

## Short version

I am working on CineMind, a self-hosted movie and TV discovery companion for people who manage their own libraries. It combines optional watch-history providers such as Plex, Trakt, Simkl and AniList with TMDb metadata, explainable recommendation filters, optional local Ollama AI and an optional MediaManager action flow.

The project is in pre-release preparation. The repository includes a clearly marked demo mode, local setup instructions and a React/FastAPI/MongoDB architecture. I am looking for a small number of people who can try a clean local installation and report concrete issues or documentation gaps.

This is not a claim of production readiness, adoption or community size. I am not buying or exchanging stars, and I will not publish usage or contributor numbers that cannot be verified from GitHub history.

Repository: https://github.com/gilbertbodyb28/cinemind
